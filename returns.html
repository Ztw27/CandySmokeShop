Returns & Refunds

Effective October 24, 2026

We want you to leave happy. Please check your items when we bring them to your car or hand them to you in the store, and let us know right away if anything is wrong.

Canceling a curbside order

Because you don't pay until pickup, you can cancel a curbside order at any time before you pay, at no cost. Just call us at (816) 326-8122.

All sales are final on these items

For health, safety, and legal reasons, we cannot accept returns of the following items once they leave the store:

  • Tobacco, cigarettes, cigars, and wraps
  • Nicotine products, including disposable vapes, pods, and e-liquids
  • Hemp, botanical, and other consumable products
  • Items that have been used, opened, or removed from their original packaging

Defective items

If an electronic item, such as a vaporizer, battery, or charger, doesn't work when you first use it, bring it back within 7 days with your receipt and all original packaging. We will test it and exchange it for the same item, or help you file a claim under the manufacturer's warranty. This does not cover damage from drops, water, misuse, or normal wear.

Glass

Glass is fragile, so please inspect it at pickup. If a glass item is cracked or damaged when we hand it to you, tell us right away and we will replace it. We cannot accept returns of glass once it has left the store.

Refunds

When a refund is approved, it is issued to your original payment method. Cash purchases are refunded in cash. A receipt is required for all returns and exchanges.

Questions

Call us at (816) 326-8122 or stop by 904 W 39th Street, Kansas City, MO 64111. We're happy to help.
