Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026

This policy explains what information Candy Smoke Shop ("we," "us," or "our") collects when you use our website, how we use it, and the choices you have. Candy Smoke Shop is located at 904 W 39th Street, Kansas City, MO 64111.

Information we collect

When you place a curbside order, we collect the information you enter in the order form:

  • Your name and phone number
  • Your vehicle description and expected arrival time
  • The items in your cart and any notes or special requests
  • Your confirmation that you are 21 or older

We do not take payments on this website, so we never collect card or bank details online. You pay in person at pickup.

Information stored in your browser. Your cart and your age confirmation are saved in your browser's local storage so they are still there when you come back. This information stays on your device until you place an order or clear your browser data.

Technical information. Like most websites, our hosting provider automatically processes basic technical data, such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you request, in order to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

We do not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

How we use your information

  • To prepare your order and bring it out to you
  • To call or text you about your order
  • To verify your age at pickup, as required by law
  • To keep sales records in our point-of-sale system
  • To prevent fraud, underage sales, and misuse of the website
  • To comply with legal obligations

We contact you only about your order. We do not send marketing messages.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for targeted advertising. We share it only with service providers that help us run the shop and website, and only as needed for them to do so:

  • Lightspeed, our point-of-sale and inventory system, where your order is recorded
  • Cloudflare, which hosts this website
  • An internal staff messaging tool that alerts our team when a new order comes in

We may also disclose information when the law requires it, such as in response to a valid court order.

Third-party content

Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts and show a map from Google Maps. When your browser loads them, Google receives your IP address and may set its own cookies, as described in Google's Privacy Policy. Links to Instagram, TikTok, and Google Maps take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies.

How long we keep information

We keep order records for as long as we need them to run our business and meet tax, accounting, and legal recordkeeping requirements.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you by calling us or visiting the store. We will honor your request unless the law requires us to keep the information. You can clear your cart and age confirmation at any time by clearing your browser data for this site.

Age restriction

This website is intended only for adults 21 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 21. If we learn that we have, we will delete it.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS). No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, however, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page.
